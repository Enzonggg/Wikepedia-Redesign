What is Wikipedia?

Wikipedia is a free, collaboratively edited encyclopedia supported by the non‑profit Wikimedia Foundation. Millions of volunteers write and improve articles every day.

Anyone can edit. Start with a topic you know, add sources, and help make knowledge available to everyone.

This is a student redesign concept focused on readability and accessibility. Not affiliated with Wikimedia Foundation.
